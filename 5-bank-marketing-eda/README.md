# Bank Marketing Campaign EDA — Python & Power BI

**Tools:** Python | Pandas | Matplotlib | Power BI

## Problem
Identify which customer segments are most likely to subscribe to a term deposit, so that marketing calls can be targeted and the cost per conversion reduced.

## Dataset
Bank marketing campaign data with customer attributes (age, job, marital status, education, balance, loans), contact details (contact type, call duration, month, day), previous campaign outcome and the subscription result. After cleaning: 44,600+ customer records.

## Approach
1. Cleaned the raw dataset (45,148 records) into an analysis-ready dataset of 44,600+ records.
2. Explored subscription patterns by contact type, previous campaign outcome and age group.
3. Built a Power BI management dashboard and report summarising the findings.

## Key Findings
- Overall subscription rate: **11.5%**
- Cellular contacts converted at **14.7%**, versus 4.1% where the contact type was unknown
- Customers with a previously successful campaign converted at **64%**
- Customers aged 60+ converted at **33%**

## Recommendation
Prioritise these segments to reduce cost per conversion.
