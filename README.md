### 1. SaaS Sales Pipeline Conversion Analysis

**Tools:** Excel | 5W / SPIN / 5C / Issue Tree Frameworks

**Problem:** The sales pipeline conversion rate at TechnoServe (a SaaS startup) dropped from 35% to 25%. Investigated root causes and recommended corrective action.

**Approach:** Applied structured consulting frameworks (5W, SPIN, 5C, Issue Tree) to break down the problem, formulate hypotheses across Customers / Company / Competitors / Collaborators / Context, and prioritize the most likely conversion drivers.

**Key Finding:** High-conversion segments identified — ERP Implementation deals, Enterprise Sellers, 0–30K opportunity sizes, and existing customers. Recommended reallocating sales effort toward these segments to recover the 10-point drop.

### 3. RSVP Movies — SQL Data Analysis

**Tools:** MySQL

**Context:** RSVP Movies is planning a global release in 2022 and needed data-driven insights to guide production and market-entry decisions.

**Dataset:** IMDB movie database across 6 tables — `movie`, `genre`, `ratings`, `names`, `director_mapping`, `role_mapping`.

**Work Done:** Answered 29 business questions using:
- Aggregate functions, GROUP BY, ORDER BY
- INNER / LEFT JOINs across multiple tables
- Subqueries and Common Table Expressions (CTEs)
- Window functions: RANK(), DENSE_RANK(), ROW_NUMBER(), LEAD()
- Date functions, conditional aggregation (CASE WHEN)

**Business Outputs:** Recommended genres, directors, actors, and production partners for RSVP's global release strategy.

### 5. Bank Marketing Campaign EDA — Python & Power BI

**Tools:** Python | Pandas | Matplotlib | Power BI

**Problem:** Identify which customer segments are most likely to subscribe to a term deposit, to reduce the cost of marketing calls.

**Approach:** Cleaned 44,600+ customer records, explored subscription patterns by contact type, previous campaign outcome and age, and built a Power BI management dashboard and report.

**Key Findings:** Overall subscription rate was 11.5%. Cellular contacts converted at 14.7%, customers with a previously successful campaign at 64%, and customers aged 60+ at 33%.

**Recommendation:** Prioritize these segments to reduce cost per conversion.
