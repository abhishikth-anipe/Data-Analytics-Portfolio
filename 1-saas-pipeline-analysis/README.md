# SaaS Sales Pipeline Conversion Analysis

**Tools:** Excel | 5W Framework | SPIN Framework | 5C Framework | Issue Tree

---

## Problem Statement

The sales pipeline conversion rate at **TechnoServe** (a tech SaaS startup) dropped from **35% at end of FY 2017-18** to **25% currently** — a 10-percentage-point decline. The objective was to understand the root cause and recommend corrective action.

---

## Approach

Structured the problem using two consulting frameworks in combination:

- **5W Framework** — Who, What, When, Where, How to frame the problem clearly
- **SPIN Framework** — Situation, Problem, Implication, Need-Payoff to understand business impact
- **5C Framework** — Customers, Company, Competitors, Collaborators, Context to cover all possible root-cause domains
- **Issue Tree** — Broke down each 5C dimension into testable hypotheses, prioritised by likelihood

---

## Key Hypotheses Generated

Across the 5C dimensions, 20+ hypotheses were formulated. Highest-priority hypotheses:

- The company is not targeting the right set of leads (P1)
- Leads believe product quality is not at par with industry trends (P2)
- The sales team is inefficient at selling the product (P2)
- Competitors offer a better product at lower price (P1)

---

## Findings & Recommendations

After analysing the opportunity dataset:

- **High-conversion segments identified:** ERP Implementation deals, Enterprise Sellers, smaller client revenue tiers (0–30K opportunity size), existing customers
- **Low-conversion drag:** New logo pipeline with large deal sizes and generalist sellers
- **Recommendation:** Reallocate sales effort toward identified high-conversion segments. Projected recovery of the 10-point drop by reducing resource spend on low-probability pipeline.
