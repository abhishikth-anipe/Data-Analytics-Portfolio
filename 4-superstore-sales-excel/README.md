# Superstore Sales Analysis — Excel

**Tools:** Advanced Excel | PivotTables | Charts | Conditional Formatting | Dashboards

---

## Dataset

- **Source:** Sample Superstore retail dataset
- **Contents:** Order-level sales data across regions, product categories, customer segments, and time periods

---

## Objectives

Analyse retail sales performance to identify high-performing regions, categories, and customer segments, and surface trends that support business decision-making.

---

## Work Done

- Data cleaning and preparation
- Sales and profit analysis by region, category, and sub-category
- Customer segment performance comparison
- Time-series trend analysis (monthly/quarterly)
- PivotTable summaries for multi-dimensional slicing
- Dashboard with charts and conditional formatting for executive visibility
